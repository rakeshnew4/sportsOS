## The Pitch (5 min)

### Problem Statement

"Sports venues (badminton, cricket, tennis) lose 20-30% of potential revenue because:
- Players book solo but courts sit empty
- No easy way to connect players for community matches
- Manual admin overhead
- Players abandon after one visit (no retention)"

### SportsOS Solution

"We solve this with three things:

1. **Hybrid Bookings** — Your solo player can open slots to the community (similar to carpool/Playo)
2. **Auto-Matching** — If you fill a court, we automatically match & charge
3. **Loyalty** — Players earn credits at YOUR venue, bringing them back"

### Business Impact

**Before SportsOS:**
- Badminton court books 3-4 times/day (50% occupancy)
- Revenue: ₹1,800-2,400/day

**After SportsOS (realistic target):**
- 6-8 bookings/day (80% occupancy)
- Revenue: ₹3,600-4,800/day
- **+50-100% revenue increase**

### How It Works for Venue Owners

"We charge **1% platform fee** on collections. You get 99%.

Example:
- Booking value: ₹600
- You receive: ₹594
- SportsOS gets: ₹6 (1%)

Weekly settlement every Monday to your bank account."

## Key Objections & Responses

### Objection: "Why would players use SportsOS instead of just calling me?"

**Response:** 

"Players WANT a community experience. Look at Uber's pool, Playo's matches — people love that.

But also: players aren't just calling you. They're scattered across WhatsApp groups, Google searches, word-of-mouth. SportsOS brings them to ONE place.

Plus, players earn credits that incentivize them to book again."

---

### Objection: "How is this different from Playo or Huddle?"

**Response:**

"Three differences:

1. **We're local** — We're not a VC-funded app that might pivot. We work with individual venues. If you're in Hyderabad, we work with Hyderabad venues.

2. **Venue control** — You own the customer relationship. Players book through us, but it's YOUR venue, YOUR loyalty program, YOUR revenue.

3. **Simplicity** — Playo is great for match discovery. We're great for venue occupancy. We're the 'Shopify for sports venues' not 'Tinder for sports.'"

---

### Objection: "What if players don't show up?"

**Response:**

"That's actually an existing problem, right? No-shows happen.

SportsOS doesn't change that. But here's what it DOES do:

- Charge upfront (no more free cancellations at last minute)
- Gamify with credits (make people want to show)
- Track repeat players (you can give VIP pricing to regulars)

If no-shows are >20%, we can discuss damage deposits."

---

### Objection: "What if there's a refund dispute?"

**Response:**

"We handle it like this:

- Player requests refund in the app
- You approve or deny
- If approved: we refund from collections
- If denied: payment stays with you
- Dispute recorded for transparency

We're the middleman only if needed."

---

### Objection: "I don't want another app to manage"

**Response:**

"I get it. You already have POS, WhatsApp groups, maybe Google Calendar.

We're not another app. We're ONE app that solves:
- Player discovery
- Booking management
- Automatic cost splitting
- Weekly payouts

Everything else stays the same. No integrations needed."
